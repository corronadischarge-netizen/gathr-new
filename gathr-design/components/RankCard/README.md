# RankCard

A ranked card with a big quiet numeral, for "Top 5 tonight" and recaps.

**Provide:** `rank`, `title`, `subtitle`, `image` and `action` (label, default "View"). `hue` is ignored in the app.

- A neutral `surface` card with a thin line. The numeral is night-600 (3:1, large text), the title ink, the subtitle ink-muted, and the button the white pill.
- Colour comes from the photo, not the card. In recap and social art, use `StatBubbles` or a SocialSlide for colour instead.
