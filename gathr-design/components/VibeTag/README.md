# VibeTag

A speech-bubble sticker from the onboarding screen ("Guestlists", "Sundowners", "Goodtime").

**Provide:** `hue`, `children` (one or two words, sentence case), `tail` (`left` or `right`) and `tilt` in degrees (−6 to 6).

- Yellow, green, pink and red use the -300 fill with dark text. Violet and blue use -500 with white text. Pink uses dark text rather than the white in the reference, so it meets contrast.
- The fill is the hue's shine: a subtle same-hue gloss with a 1px top highlight. The tail is one clean triangle in the base hue, tucked under the pill.
- Expression zone only: onboarding, empty states, social, posters, web. Don't use it as an app filter.
