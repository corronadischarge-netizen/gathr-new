# Icon

UI icons come from **Lucide** (lucide.dev, ISC licence): outline icons on a 24px grid, drawn at a 1.75 stroke with round caps and joins. The icon takes the colour of its parent.

**Provide:** `name` (the exact Lucide name), `size` (16, 20 or 24), `filled` for the solid version (see IconFilled), and `label` when the icon stands alone and has meaning.

**Need one that isn't here?**
1. Find it on lucide.dev/icons.
2. Copy its exact name.
3. Add it to the bundle's icon set, unchanged.

Never draw your own icons, redraw or retouch a Lucide icon, or mix in another set.

**Rules:**
- **Icons are for UI** (navigation, actions, states). The 3D stickers are for expression; never use one in place of the other.
- **Outline by default; filled means "on".** Use `filled` only for an active or selected state: the current tab, saved, booked, liked, the selected pin. Never mix outline and filled icons in one row for decoration.
- **Sizes:** 20px in buttons and rows, 24px in the tab bar, 16px inside pills and meta.
- **Pair with a word** wherever there's room. Icon-only buttons need a `label` and a 44px hit area.
- **No brand logos.** Lucide has no brand icons, so source social logos separately when needed.
