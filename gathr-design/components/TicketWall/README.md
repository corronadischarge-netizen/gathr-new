# TicketWall

A showcase of tickets as they look on "Your tickets" and in shares: fanned and tilted, with friends' reactions around them.

Built from:
- `Ticket` at scales 0.84 to 1.08, tilted −9° to 14°
- one `ReactionBubble` and one `CommentBubble` (emoji come from people)
- a `VibeTag` ("Got the ticket")
- a still `Bloom`
- `Button`s: Share ticket and Add to wallet

Rules:
- The front ticket is upright. Only one sticker bobs (on the hosted ticket).
- Use at most four tickets in a stack.
