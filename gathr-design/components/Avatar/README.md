# Avatar

A person's display picture in a circle, or their centred initials on a grey disc. Optional vibe sticker on the corner.

**Provide:** `image` (their photo), `name` (alt text and initials), `size` in px (28 in stacks, 48 in the top bar), and `sticker` (their chosen 3D object from assets/Stickers).

- **Vibe sticker:** one object per person, picked in their profile. It sits on the bottom-right corner at about half the avatar's size, tilted. It replaces the retired shape-cut avatars.
- No photo: initials (one or two letters, DM Sans 600), optically centred. Never a silhouette or generated face.
- In a stack, each avatar gets a 2px ring in the surface colour.
