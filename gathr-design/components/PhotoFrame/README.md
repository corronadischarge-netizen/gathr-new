# PhotoFrame

A collage photo with a thin glass edge, a radius-md corner and a slight tilt, for onboarding and social collages.

**Provide:** `image`, `width`, `height`, `tilt` (−8 to 8) (`hue` is ignored; empty frames show the neutral placeholder).

- A collage holds 3–4 frames that overlap, with VibeTags and 3D stickers pinned to their edges.

- Edge: a thin glass stroke (1px white at 25% plus a top highlight) and a soft shadow. Never a thick white border.
