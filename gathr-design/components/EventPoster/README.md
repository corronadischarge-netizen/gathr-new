# EventPoster

The event poster at A-series proportion (1080 × 1527 on screen, or A3/A2 in print).

**Provide:** `recipe` (the bloom), `hue` (the pill colour), `altHue`, `kicker`, `name`, `series`, `date`, `time`, `venue`, `support` and `scale`.

- Hierarchy runs: the name (organisers keep their own spelling, no full stop; only gathr-hosted `occasion` posters take lowercase + the yellow full stop), then the pill chain (series, date → time, venue), then the support line.
- Pill text is sentence case: "Sat 04.10", "F Bar, Pune".
- The ground is black by default. Add `occasion` (gathr-hosted nights, big city nights) for a still bloom in the event's recipe. The name sits in the dark upper half.
