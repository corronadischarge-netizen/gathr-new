Outside the app, colour plays. The Wrapped card stays solid violet: a brand moment inside the app. Keep it to **violet + yellow + one more hue** per piece. The one exception is the `peak` recipe (five lights), kept for launches, NYE and the echo pattern.

The story behind these rules (logo, lights, type, pattern, tickets and social) lives in the [gathr brand book](https://claude.ai/artifact/UpE3wvgwzYqppeY1gwwVGk).

## Social

- Cover: a real photo or black, wordmark top-left, the pillar `VibeTag`, a display headline with the yellow full stop. No 3D objects on social slides; type and colour carry them.
- Body slides: one idea, 25 words max. End slide: violet field, one action.
- `EchoPattern` is the brand pattern for end slides, share cards, merch and backdrops. The vibe-tag bubble echoes out from the logo, with edges glowing in a Bloom recipe (`peak`, `uv`, `sundowner`, `comedown`).

## Tickets

The `Ticket` is built like a social asset:
- **Layout:** wordmark and vibe tag on top, then the name, a "day date → time" pill row and the venue. A centred code sits on the stub.
- **Keep it minimal:** no images, no big date, no italics and no 3D stickers.
- **The code** is printed in the ticket's ink with no tile: black on light grounds, white on violet, blue and night. It stays at 4.5:1 or more on a flat stub.
- **Colour pairs:**
  - violet, blue and red grounds pop with yellow
  - pink, green and yellow pop with violet
  - gathr-hosted nights go black with a still bloom and the yellow full stop
- On shares and "Your tickets", fan them with friends' reactions around them (`TicketWall`).

## Print and posters

- `EventPoster`: black by default; a still bloom only for occasions (print from assets/Gradients). Name huge, facts in a `PillChain`, 24-hour time.
- One event, one light: techno violet, disco and house pink, rooftops yellow, after-hours blue.
- Neon yellow and pink need fluorescent spot inks (Pantone 803, 806). QR codes 20 mm+.

## Web

- `SiteHeader` glass pill, a black hero with the onboarding collage (photos, stickers, vibe tags), real app screens as proof.
- Gradient only for launches. One brand button per viewport.

## Occasions

Launches, gathr-hosted nights, NYE / Holi / Diwali, recaps, first run: this is when the `Bloom` and the sticker pack come out together.
