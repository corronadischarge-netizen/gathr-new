# Voice

*(Image: Do and don't copy pairs. See the live design system.)*

**Why:** human words answer "how busy" and "what will it cost" faster than numbers. "Packed by 11", not "82%" ([brand book](https://claude.ai/artifact/UpE3wvgwzYqppeY1gwwVGk) §07).

gathr talks like the friend who's already inside: short, dry, specific. Based on the [Gen Z research](https://claude.ai/code/artifact/31803803-474e-44e2-a60b-0a20e995c8c7).

## Rules

- **Specific beats hype.** "Packed by 11" beats "amazing vibes". Time, crowd, sound, price.
- **Sentence case**, including the pillar names ("The mirror"). Buttons start with a verb. No exclamation marks.
- **No full stop at the end of a UI message.** To Gen Z it reads as annoyed. The yellow full stop belongs to gathr's display headlines only.
- **Context, then a nudge.** Name the moment, add proof, stop.
- **Slang light:** one everyday word per screen at most ("lowkey", "sorted"). Hinglish only in social and pushes.
- **Emoji come from people** (`ReactionBubble`, comments), never from the UI.
- **Local:** "KP", "Baner", "Kalyani Nagar".

## Booked so far

The scale says **how full a night will be**, from bookings on gathr against a cap verified by gathr. It never describes the vibe; that's the "Vibe" row in The room.

| Level | Booked | Line |
|---|---|---|
| Early | under 25% of the cap | Few booked yet. Plenty of room |
| Filling up | 25–59% | Easy to get in |
| Buzzing | 60–84% | Good crowd. Short wait at the door |
| Packed | 85–99% | Peak night. Expect a queue |
| Full | sold out | Sold out. Only list and pass holders get in |

- Show people, never a percentage: "**412 of 480** spots booked". "Never a percentage" covers crowd and Booked so far only; host stats may use them ("83% checked in"). Under 50 bookings, show the line instead of the count.
- Trust line: "Based on bookings on gathr. Cap of 480 verified by gathr. Packed means 85% or more is booked. Updated 6:40 pm".
- Walk-in venues with no cap: "120 booked tonight", no level.
- Never "Quiet" or "Dead": they read as the vibe.
- **How we count** (official copy, under TonightSoFar): "How we count: Booked so far is bookings on gathr against tonight's cap, verified by gathr. Walk-ins aren't counted, so the room can feel a little fuller".

## Updates

- The title says what happened, in plain words: "Euphoria was sent back", "Twin Strings: few left", "Ishaan wants to be friends".
- One meta line, about 32 characters, with the time at the end: "Add the night's genre · 2h".
- Buttons are one word where possible: Fix, Book, Pay. Accept and Decline for people.
- Never show internal handles ("new_host", "duplicate") or full stops at the end.
- After Decline: "Request declined. Ishaan won't be told", with Undo.
