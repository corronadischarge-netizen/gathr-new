# What changed since the last package (releases 19 and 20, 11 Oct 2026)

The last package was release 18 (10 Oct). Two releases have landed since. Work through this list after anything still open from `CHANGES-release-18.md`.

Colours and spacing tokens did not change (`tokens.json` is identical), so `tokens.css` is the same as last time.

## Release 19: every text size on the ladder
Every size of UI text is now **32, 20, 16, 15, 13 or 11**. No 12, 14, 17, 18, 22 or 24.

These moved:
| Component | Before | Now |
|---|---|---|
| Chip label, FeatureCard body, RankCard button, CommentBubble, SiteHeader links | 14 | 15 |
| TagPill, MapPin labels, media label, ShapeAvatar captions | 12 | 13 |
| VibeTag | 17 | 16 |
| DotGrid number | 48 | 32 |
| ReactionBubble | 22 | 20 |

The `label` style now covers three sizes: buttons 16; chips, small buttons and nav links 15; tags, map pins and captions 13. Weight 500–600.

Not UI text, so not on the ladder: canvases drawn at 1080px and scaled (SocialSlide, EventPoster, Ticket), the RankCard ghost numeral, avatar initials and emoji.

**In the app:** search for every `font-size` and snap anything off the ladder. In `components/bundle.css` the corrected sizes are the overrides at the end of the file; the earlier rules still show the old sizes, so the last rule wins.

## Release 20: Updates (notifications)
A new screen and four new components: **`Update`**, **`UpdateGroup`**, **`UpdateLead`** and **`Toast`**. Props are in `components/index.d.ts`; full rules in `components/Update/README.md`; example screen in `components/Update/preview.html`.

- **Two groups:** "Needs you", then "Earlier". No filters, no trackers.
- **Each row:** a lead (a night's 3D object, a person's photo, or gathr's icon tinted by meaning), a title saying what happened (15/600), one meta line (13px, about 32 characters) with the time at the end: "Fri 9 Oct · ₹1,299 · 5h".
- **Four kinds, and the button is the real answer:**
  - `task`: you must do something → one white button ("Fix", "Pay")
  - `chance`: optional and time-sensitive → one violet button ("Book")
  - `decide`: someone waits on a yes or no → Decline (✕) and Accept (✓), equal size, side by side
  - `info`: just news → no button, a chevron; one muted line in Earlier
- Only "Needs you" rows have buttons; **three answers on screen at most** (a Decline/Accept pair counts as one).
- **One row per night:** sent, in review and live replace each other.
- **Declining is quiet:** the other person isn't told. Show a `Toast` "Request declined. Ishaan won't be told" with Undo for 5 seconds.
- More than two friend requests collapse into one row: "3 friend requests".
- Unread "Needs you" rows sit on a card; Earlier rows are flat and muted.
- **Spacing (4px grid):** row padding 16 · lead to text 16 · title to meta 4 · text to button 16 · rows 8 apart · group head to rows 12 · groups 32 apart · gutter 16 · page title to first group 24. Earlier rows: 8/12 padding, 36px lead.
- **Copy:** buttons one word where possible (Fix, Book, Pay, Accept, Decline). Never show internal handles like "new_host" or "duplicate". No full stops at the end.

**In the app:** if there's a notifications or activity screen, rebuild it to this. If there isn't one, add it with sample rows (labelled sample), reached from the bell in the top bar.

## Also new in this package
- Every component now has its `preview.html` (last time some were missing).
- The `api/` pages and `manifest.json` are left out: they're generated by the design system page and were out of date. Use `index.d.ts` and the component READMEs.
- Previews load images from the live system (`/_blob/...` addresses). Those won't load inside the app; they're there to show markup and layout. The real 3D objects and photos aren't in this package yet.
